Which Degrees?

Everything you need to know to graduate and where to study:

Higher education: disciplines such as Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, Physics or related fields, specializations, master's and/or doctorates in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science or Robotics. Researchers typically pursue these high-level degrees.

PUC Campinas

It offers courses in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. A pioneering course in the area and aligned with the demands of the job market. Experienced and specialized professionals who guarantee complete training, developing fundamental skills and competencies for professional success.

Federal University of Paraíba (UFPB)

The Bachelor's degree in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence is offered by the Informatics Center of UFPB, has a workload of 3000 hours and a duration of four years. The curriculum includes disciplines such as Theory of Computation, Vector Calculus and Analytic Geometry, Discrete Mathematics, Structured Programming, Operations Research, Information Retrieval, Statistical Data Mining, among others.

Federal University of Goiás (UFG)

The Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence at the Federal University of Goiás (UFG) lasts four years. The course is offered full-time and the university offers 40 spots per class.
