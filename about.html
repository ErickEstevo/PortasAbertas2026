Role

What an Artificial Intelligence Researcher does:

An Artificial Intelligence Researcher creates from scratch new AI models, techniques, and applications. The work is pure innovation, focused on cutting-edge research within universities, laboratories, and technology giants.

Main professions

What are the main professions:

Data Scientist

Mines, organizes, and translates mountains of data into practical answers. This professional creates predictive models and uses artificial intelligence to fish out hidden patterns, clearing the path for strategic decisions.

Machine Learning Engineer

Creates and deploys machine learning algorithms. The focus here is to build intelligent systems that learn on their own from the information they receive, evolving their own performance without the need for constant intervention.

Prompt Engineer

Designs, tests, and refines commands to extract the best possible results from generative AIs. Basically, they translate what we want into the language that the machine understands.

Salaries

A Data Scientist earns on average R$ 11,099.46 per month; a Machine Learning Engineer earns on average R$ 8,671 per month; and a Prompt Engineer averages R$ 15,000 per month.
